## Current Discord game wording

### Pokémon trade
- Empty offer: `🔴 <user> — selecting a card`
- Card selected: `🟡 <user> — <card> (<set>-<number>) • <rarity>`
- Accepted: `🟢 <user> — <card> (<set>-<number>) • <rarity> • accepted`
- Ready prompt: `Both cards are selected. Each player must review and press Accept Trade.`
- Selection prompt: `Use Choose My Card to open your collection, then select an eligible card.`
- Completion: `<initiator> received <target card>. <target> received <initiator card>.`

### Collection
- Empty: `Your shared Pokémon collection is empty. Use !pack to see the available sets.`
- Populated: `Your Discord and Twitch cards share one Pokédex. Rarest pull: <card> (<rarity>).`

### Gamble/economy
The exact result wording is generated by the Discord gamble settlement path and standardized embed renderer. The canonical wallet rule in code is: the stake leaves spendable XP; a win refills spendable XP up to lifetime XP; overflow above lifetime is compressed 10:1 before increasing lifetime rank.
